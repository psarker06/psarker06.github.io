# Research Project Images

Place research project images in this directory.

## Required Images:

1. **mr-training.jpg** - Mixed Reality Training System visualization
2. **ergonomic-assessment.jpg** - Ergonomic assessment system
3. **biomechanics.jpg** - Biomechanical analysis visualization
4. **cognitive-workload.jpg** - Cognitive workload assessment
5. **intervention.jpg** - Ergonomic intervention examples
6. **wearables.jpg** - Wearable sensor technology

## Image Specifications:

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Recommended size**: 1200x600 pixels (2:1 aspect ratio)
- **Maximum file size**: 1MB
- **Style**: Professional, high-quality research images, diagrams, or system screenshots

## Tips:

- Use clear, professional images that illustrate your research
- Can include system diagrams, equipment photos, or data visualizations
- Ensure images are not pixelated when displayed at full width
- Consider using images from your publications or presentations

## Placeholder Images:

Until you add your own images, you can:
1. Use stock photos related to ergonomics and workplace safety
2. Create simple diagrams or flowcharts
3. Use screenshots from your research systems
4. Include photos from your lab or field studies (with appropriate permissions)
