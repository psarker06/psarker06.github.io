# Research Project Images

Place research project images in this directory. The "Featured Research Projects"
section on the research page is currently text-only (no images supplied yet) — add
an `<img>` back into `_pages/research.md` for a project once you have a real photo,
diagram, or system screenshot for it.

Real lab equipment photos live in `equipment/` and are already wired into the
"Research Facilities & Equipment" section of `_pages/research.md`.

## Image Specifications

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Recommended size**: 1200x600 pixels (2:1 aspect ratio)
- **Maximum file size**: 1MB
- **Style**: Professional, high-quality research images, diagrams, or system screenshots
